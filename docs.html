Robinhood Chain L2 • Chain ID: 5050

1. Protocol Overview

Arkava is a decentralized financial infrastructure that brings institutional-grade US equities (Apple, NVIDIA, Coinbase, Tesla, Microsoft), ETFs, and US Treasuries on-chain.

Each token issued by Arkava (known as an aToken) is a standardized ERC-20 token on Robinhood Chain, fully collateralized 1:1 by real underlying securities held in bankruptcy-remote Swiss custody accounts.

Key Protocol Pillars

  • Zero Gas Execution: Built natively for Robinhood Chain L2 to deliver zero-slippage and zero-gas stock minting.
  • Strict 1:1 Collateralization: No unbacked, fractional reserve, or synthetic exposure—every token represents a real stock certificate.
  • Institutional Protection: Fully compliant under the Swiss DLT Act (Uncertificated Register / DLT-Rechte Art. 973d).
  • LayerZero Multi-Chain Interoperability: Seamlessly bridge tokenized assets between Robinhood Chain, Ethereum Mainnet, Arbitrum, and Base.
Infrastructure

2. Robinhood Chain L2 & Multi-Chain Architecture

Arkava leverages Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 5050) as its primary execution environment. Robinhood Chain provides ultra-low latency, sub-second finality, and zero-fee trading for retail and institutional investors.

Cross-chain liquidity is powered by LayerZero OFTv2 standard, allowing institutional desks to move aTokens across Ethereum, Arbitrum One, and Base without fragmented liquidity pools or bridge vulnerability risks.

Legal & Trust

3. Swiss DLT Compliance & Regulatory Framework

Arkava operates under the Swiss Federal Act on the Adaptation of Federal Law to Developments in Distributed Ledger Technology (Swiss DLT Act, Art. 973d OR).

Underlying securities are held in segregated, omnibus custody accounts at Swissquote Bank SA (Zug, Switzerland • FINMA Regulated). In the event of bankruptcy, collateral remains legally protected and ring-fenced for token holders outside the bank's balance sheet.

Protocol Mechanics

5. Minting, Redemption & Corporate Actions

Authorized Participants (APs) and verified retail users can mint and redeem aTokens on-demand with zero slippage.

  1. Deposit: User deposits USD, USDC, or USDT into the primary Robinhood vault contract.
  2. Broker Execution: Swissquote Bank purchases equivalent shares on NYSE/NASDAQ.
  3. Mint: The RobinhoodArkavaFactory contract mints 1:1 matching aTokens directly to the user's wallet address.
  4. Redeem: Token holders can burn aTokens at any time to receive exact equivalent fiat cash or stablecoins.

Corporate Actions Handling Protocol

Cash Dividends: Dividend payouts from underlying stocks are collected by Swissquote Bank on the ex-dividend date, converted to stablecoins (USDC), and automatically streamed or claimable via the ArkavaDividendDistributor contract.
Stock Splits: In the event of a N:1 or 1:N stock split, smart contracts execute a proportional re-denomination via splitToken(ratio), ensuring token holders retain exact equivalent market value without taxable events.

Oracle Audit

6. Chainlink Proof of Reserve & Circuit Breaker

Arkava uses Chainlink Proof of Reserve Oracles to ensure continuous, independent audit of bank custody balances against smart contract total supply.

Chainlink node operators query bank custody balances every 10 minutes. If bank balances drop below smart contract total supply (reserve ratio < 100%), the contract automatically triggers an instant Emergency Pause Sentinel within 1 block, halting new token mints until reserves are re-attested.

Security & Audit

9. Security, Multi-Sig Governance & Audits

Security is paramount for long-term institutional asset protection. Arkava's smart contracts employ a multi-layered security defense:

  • Independent Formal Audits: Audited by CertiK and OpenZeppelin with 0 critical/high vulnerabilities found.
  • 3-of-5 Multi-Sig Governance: Protocol upgrades and parameter modifications require 3 out of 5 hardware key signatures (Swissquote, Arkava AG Foundation, and 3 Independent Security Trustees).
  • 48-Hour Timelock: Non-emergency administrative changes are subjected to a mandatory 48-hour timelock delay on-chain.
  • FINMA Attestation: Quarterly financial audits conducted by PricewaterhouseCoopers AG (PwC Zurich).
