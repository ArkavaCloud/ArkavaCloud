Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers on security, Swiss regulation, collateral custody, and token trading.

How does Arkava guarantee 1:1 real stock collateralization?
↓
Every aToken minted on Robinhood Chain is strictly backed 1:1 by real stock shares purchased on US exchanges (NYSE/NASDAQ) and held in segregated omnibus accounts at Swissquote Bank SA. Automated Chainlink Proof of Reserve oracles audit bank balances against token supply every 10 minutes.
How do I redeem my aTokens back to USD cash or stablecoins?
↓
Token holders can redeem aTokens anytime via the Arkava Mint & Swap interface. When a redemption request is placed, the contract burns the tokens, Swissquote Bank liquidates the equivalent underlying shares, and USD cash or USDC/USDT is wired directly to your designated account or Web3 wallet.
What legal protections exist if Swissquote Bank faces bankruptcy?
↓
Under Article 973d of the Swiss DLT Act (*Ledger-Based Securities*), underlying collateral is bankruptcy-remote (*segregated asset pool*). In the event of custodian insolvency, collateralized securities remain legally ring-fenced and belong exclusively to aToken holders, entirely separate from bank liquidation assets.
Why is Robinhood Chain chosen as the primary Layer-2 execution network?
↓
Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 5050) provides zero-gas execution, sub-second transaction finality, and seamless compatibility with mobile self-custody wallets, making it the ideal L2 ecosystem for friction-free stock tokenization and trading.
Are Arkava aTokens transferable on decentralized exchanges (Uniswap)?
↓
Yes. Arkava aTokens follow standard ERC-20 and ERC-2612 (Permit) specifications, allowing permissionless transfer, collateralization in DeFi lending protocols, and liquidity pool trading across Robinhood Chain and EVM networks.

Swiss DLT Legal Prospectus

Swiss DLT Act Compliance Registration
1. Swiss Regulatory Framework

Arkava tokens are issued under Article 973d of the Swiss Code of Obligations (*Ledger-Based Securities*). Tokens represent uncertificated rights tied directly to underlying assets.

2. Custody & Insolvency Protection

Underlying securities are held in segregated, bankruptcy-remote accounts at Swissquote Bank SA (FINMA Regulated). Assets do not form part of the custodian's balance sheet.

3. ISIN Registration

Each tokenized asset corresponds to a registered ISIN (International Securities Identification Number) cross-referenced in the on-chain registry.
